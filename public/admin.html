Quotes

Quotes are estimates for work that hasn't been booked yet, emailed to the customer automatically as soon as you save one. Once a customer accepts, convert the quote into a scheduled job and/or an invoice.

Invoices

Invoices are created as drafts, then emailed to the customer once you tap Send. The email includes a Pay now button for online card/bank payments, which marks the invoice paid automatically. Use Mark Paid yourself only for payments that came in another way (cash, check, a card run elsewhere).

Transactions

Every paid invoice -- in-person/phone payments you recorded yourself, and online payments collected automatically through Stripe.

Expenses

Materials, insurance, rent, or any other business cost -- logged here so Net Profit on the Reports tab reflects what you actually spent, not just labor.

Inventory

Inventory

A quote alone never puts anything "on hold" -- items show up on hold once a pull sheet is built for the job or it's turned into an invoice, and come out of stock for good once that pull sheet is fulfilled or the invoice is paid in full. Turn tracking on for a catalog item and set its starting quantity/cost in the Settings tab above.

ItemAvailableOn holdUnit costTotal value

Pull sheets

Picking lists built from a job's inventory-tracked items. Building one from a quote (or a standalone one) is what puts those items on hold; building one from an invoice doesn't add to the count since the invoice already reserved them. Marking one fulfilled removes those quantities from stock for good.

Catalog

Recurring charges you can drop into a quote or invoice with one tap.

Import an Excel (.xlsx) or CSV file with a Name column. Unit Price, Quantity (on hand), and Unit Cost columns are all optional -- if a row has a Quantity or Cost, inventory tracking turns on for that item automatically. Rows matching an existing item's name are skipped.

Inventory settings

Turn on tracking for any catalog item, set its quantity on hand and cost, and set the low inventory level that triggers an alert. Editing quantity on hand here is also how you record a restock.
