Quotes

Quotes are estimates for work that hasn't been booked yet, emailed to the customer automatically as soon as you save one. Once a customer accepts, convert the quote into a scheduled job and/or an invoice.

Invoices

Invoices are created as drafts, then emailed to the customer once you tap Send. The email includes a Pay now button for online card/bank payments, which marks the invoice paid automatically. Use Mark Paid yourself only for payments that came in another way (cash, check, a card run elsewhere).

Transactions

Every paid invoice -- in-person/phone payments you recorded yourself, and online payments collected automatically through Stripe.

Expenses

Materials, insurance, rent, or any other business cost -- logged here so Net Profit on the Reports tab reflects what you actually spent, not just labor.

Reports

Labor hours, invoiced totals, paid totals, and profit for a time range.

Get paid online

Connect your own Stripe account so customer payments on your invoices go straight to your bank account, not ours. Stripe's own processing fees apply (2.9%+30¢ card / 0.8% bank transfer), plus a 0.5% platform fee per payment.
